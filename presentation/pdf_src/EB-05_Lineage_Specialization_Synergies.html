Scope: One named optional sidegrade for each of the 360 lineage–class pairings. Each technique adapts the Tier I skill of one specialization of that class. That pairing is not a recommendation: every lineage keeps equal access to all three specializations. These are not new specializations, not automatic racial bonuses and not exclusive powers.

Changed in v1.1: recommended specializations removed; each technique's exchange is split into a player-facing Effect and Limits; Crystalforged prerequisite Resonant Brace is now Lattice Brace. Technique names are kept as lineage flavour: techniques on the same specialization share one effect.

Uniform synergy rule (applies to every technique below)

  1. Eligibility: Own the named lineage, play the indicated class and specialization, and have that specialization's Tier I skill and the named racial Tier II skill.
  2. Cost: Gain one synergy training slot by completing an advanced Guild training objective. It grants no extra combat action or daily pool. Fill or retrain the slot only between adventures. Guild objectives and RP/SP awards must not depend on biome.
  3. One active technique: Equip at most one. It is an alternative mode of the named Tier I specialization skill, not a second activation. Normal actions, resources, range, checks, targets and uses still apply.
  4. Exchange, never stack: The Limits line says what the original Tier I skill gives up. No extra damage, healing, reactions, companion turns, runes, movement, targets, flight, immunity or free spells.
  5. Other specializations: A player on one of the class's other two specializations can take the identical-cost Open Discipline Adaptation: once per use of that specialization's Tier I skill, replace its combat advantage with a small investigation or navigation cue tied to one of the player's racial Tier II skills. No bonus attack or movement; same action and uses. Themed techniques for those pairings may be written later.
  6. Effect size: Effects are qualitative until dice, ranges and checks are approved. A GM may allow a small relevant clue or equivalent positional alternative, with no guaranteed success.
  7. World safety: No instant kills, forced player actions, compulsory spirit truth, unrestricted flight, teleportation or movement, unlimited traps or companions, or material-based poison or healing immunities.

How to read: Class → specialization adapted · Technique · Racial prerequisite · Theme · Effect · Limits. Every technique also carries the standard limit: Needs one synergy training slot · one active technique · no extra actions or resources.

  • Record the lineage skill and its changed function in the combat log; GMs should see both the original effect forfeited and the alternate effect used.

Example: Wolfkin Wildstrider → Beastwarden

A Wolfkin who owns the racial Tier II skill Scent Ledger, the Beastwarden Tier I skill Coordinated Pounce, and the synergy-training slot may equip Packtrail Bond. On a valid companion command, they can have the companion investigate or signal a relevant scent trail instead of creating Coordinated Pounce's attack opening. The companion does not act twice. This adaptation is useful for tracking but does not give a free combat buff.

Rules and interpretation

  1. Race and lineage never lock a class: All 60 lineages may select any of Vanguard, Wildstrider, Shadowblade, Luminary, Arcanist, or Resonant.
  2. Specializations also remain open: A lineage may choose any of the three advanced specializations of its class, with equal standing. The listed specialization is the one its synergy technique adapts; it is not a recommendation, prerequisite, bonus, restricted build or required route.
  3. Themes are design hooks, not new granted powers: The phrase after the dash describes how that lineage might express the listed specialization. It does not add free actions, bonuses, damage, spell access, companions, or skills. Any future mechanical variant must be costed, capped and tested before use.
  4. Core progression is unchanged: Four free starting class abilities; 12 universal class skills per class; specialization eligibility proposed after five SP in universal skills and a Guild trial; one active specialization; specialization trait plus four sequential advanced skills. Guild ranks F–SS remain independent of character skill progression.
  5. Future racial skill trees are independent: A lineage has its own innate/learned identity and future racial abilities regardless of chosen specialization. Cross-system interactions can be designed as balanced sidegrades rather than cumulative free buffs.
  6. World consistency: Human regional heritages remain learned traditions; Drakari scale color is cosmetic; Sylph and Skyborn wings are limited to approved gliding/wing-assisted movement; Tidekin have land-use options; Forgeborn do not gain unstated immunities or self-repair.

Future balance and implementation requirements

  • No extra power from a pairing alone. A highly thematic choice is not more effective than the alternatives.
  • Avoid a 360-skill inflation problem. Prefer named visual variants, situational prompts, or clearly priced sidegrades to every pairing having a free additional signature ability.
  • Preserve cross-terrain viability. Aquatic, aerial, underground, heat/cold, and woodland hooks must not prevent general adventure gameplay outside those terrains.
  • Avoid stereotype locks. Background, race, and lineage cannot dictate morality, personality, religious affiliation, or class choice.
  • Respect action economy. Beasts, echoes, runes, traps, and coordinated attacks do not produce unlimited extra actions.
  • Tag mechanical revisions. If a future lineage synergy affects dice, range, duration, healing, movement, or resources, store it as a separately reviewed ability/version with prerequisites and cross-GM text.
