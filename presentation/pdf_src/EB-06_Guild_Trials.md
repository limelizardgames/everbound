**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed / Not yet playtested  
**Scope:** 18 standardized specialization trials · 12 base races · 60 lineage customization hooks  
**Companion documents:** Everbound Class & Specialization Compendium; Everbound Racial Lineage Compendium; Everbound Character Progression Compendium

> **Design intent:** An adventurer earns a specialization by demonstrating how they think and act, not simply by reaching a level. All lineages can complete the same trial for a given specialization. Lineage changes presentation, environmental opportunities, and flavor—not the required objective, base difficulty, or reward.

---

## 1. Guild Trial Framework

### Eligibility and reward

| Component | Proposed rule |
|---|---|
| Available trials | 18, one per established class specialization |
| Entry requirement | At least **5 Skill Points (SP)** invested in the adventurer's universal class tree |
| Trial duration | Approximately **20–30 minutes** |
| Starting class | Remains Vanguard, Wildstrider, Shadowblade, Luminary, Arcanist, or Resonant |
| Specialization | Player chooses one of their class's three approved specializations |
| Skills permitted | Existing class and racial abilities; **do not require** abilities from the specialization being tested |
| Primary reward | The selected specialization's **Discipline Trait**, plus access to its four-tier advanced skill tree |
| Guild rank | Remains separate from specialization certification (F–SS rank is not a prerequisite) |
| Failure | Feedback and remedial mission or later retry; no character loss or erased progress |
| Assistance | Party members may help, but the **applicant** is evaluated individually |
| GM authority | Any authorized GM may administer an approved standardized trial |

### Three-stage structure

1. **Demonstration:** Show competence with starting-class skills and the underlying challenge.
2. **Complication:** Respond when the initial plan is disrupted.
3. **Final Test:** Make a consequential decision that reveals the specialization's philosophy.

### Standard assessment rubric

Evaluate four criteria:

1. **Objective:** Achieved the trial's central mission.
2. **Class Mastery:** Used existing class skills in a meaningful way.
3. **Adaptability:** Responded to the unexpected complication.
4. **Judgment:** Demonstrated the discipline's defining principle.

**Proposed pass requirement:** Satisfy at least **3 of 4 criteria**, including **Judgment**. Use the same four-criterion form at every table. Narratively creative solutions may satisfy a criterion without following the expected tactic. These are guidelines to test, not finalized numerical game mechanics.

### Fairness and accessibility

- The **core objective and pass criteria do not change** for different races or lineages.
- Lineage-specific clues, props, routes, and NPC responses must provide **comparable opportunities**, not automatic successes.
- Each trial must remain completable with the character's **already unlocked abilities**, without depending on an advanced specialization technique.
- For water, confined passages, elevated routes, or gliding, provide a fair alternative where appropriate.
- Preserve approved race anatomy and limitations: **no unrestricted Sylph/Skyborn flight, no automatic Drakari breath weapons, no assumptions of Forgeborn immunities**.
- Failure should create a useful story moment and a path to retry, not a permanent lockout.

## 2. The 18 Standardized Specialization Trials

### Vanguard

#### 01. Guardian — The Last Lantern

**Focus:** Protection and responsibility.  
**Premise:** Escort a wounded Guild apprentice carrying a ceremonial lantern through a hostile training route.

1. **Demonstration:** Protect the apprentice across two checkpoints, using positioning or defensive class techniques.
2. **Complication:** A simulated ambush separates the apprentice from the planned escape route.
3. **Final Test:** The exit becomes accessible, but the apprentice remains in danger. The applicant must secure their protection rather than simply flee.

**Passing objective:** Bring the apprentice to safety while keeping the lantern intact or recoverable. Creative defensive solutions count as much as absorbing attacks.

#### 02. Berserker — The Crimson Bell

**Focus:** Controlled aggression and restraint.  
**Premise:** Defeat a progressively more difficult arena champion in a supervised Guild training ring.

1. **Demonstration:** Face a heavily armored opponent and maintain offensive pressure.
2. **Complication:** The arena changes, forcing adaptation instead of reliance on raw aggression.
3. **Final Test:** A civilian actor enters the danger zone while the opponent remains vulnerable.

**Passing objective:** Overcome the champion without harming the civilian. The defining lesson is knowing when to restrain overwhelming force.

#### 03. Warlord — The Broken Banner

**Focus:** Tactical leadership.  
**Premise:** Lead three novice Guild volunteers across a simulated battlefield to recover a fallen banner.

1. **Demonstration:** Organize volunteers to secure two tactical positions.
2. **Complication:** A messenger delivers incorrect intelligence, requiring a revised plan.
3. **Final Test:** Recover the banner while arranging a safe withdrawal for the volunteers.

**Passing objective:** Communicate, adapt, and lead effectively rather than simply inflicting personal damage.

### Wildstrider

#### 04. Beastwarden — The First Bond

**Focus:** Animal trust and cooperative action.  
**Premise:** Earn the trust of a Guild-approved animal and complete a short joint task.

1. **Demonstration:** Approach the animal, recognize its behavior, and build trust without coercion.
2. **Complication:** A disturbance frightens the animal or interrupts its task.
3. **Final Test:** Locate and retrieve a missing Guild token together without endangering the animal.

**Passing objective:** Show patience, communication, and mutual cooperation. When appropriate, the animal may become the character's first bonded companion after certification; use the balanced companion rules established for Beastwarden.

#### 05. Deadeye — The Four Bells

**Focus:** Precision and responsible target selection.  
**Premise:** Complete four progressively more complex ranged challenges.

1. **Demonstration:** Strike two clearly marked targets from different positions.
2. **Complication:** A moving target appears behind partial cover while distractions interfere with timing.
3. **Final Test:** Identify and strike the correct bell among decoys without hitting a protected target.

**Passing objective:** Display accuracy, recognition, and restraint. Any suitable ranged weapon is permitted; a bow is not mandatory.

#### 06. Trailblazer — The Lost Road

**Focus:** Navigation, survival, and expedition leadership.  
**Premise:** Guide a small supply expedition to a Guild outpost through unfamiliar territory.

1. **Demonstration:** Choose a route using maps, landmarks, and environmental clues.
2. **Complication:** A fallen bridge, blocked trail, or weather event invalidates the original route.
3. **Final Test:** Bring the expedition to safety while preserving essential supplies.

**Passing objective:** Reach safety through sound judgment, resource management, and navigation.

### Shadowblade

#### 07. Assassin — The Quiet Contract

**Focus:** Precision, preparation, and discretion.  
**Premise:** Infiltrate a guarded compound to retrieve a contract seal from a designated training target.

1. **Demonstration:** Study guard movements and identify a viable route.
2. **Complication:** The intended target relocates or is replaced by a decoy.
3. **Final Test:** Retrieve the seal and escape without harming an uninvolved servant or setting off a major alarm.

**Passing objective:** Complete the assignment with precision and discretion. **Killing is not required.**

#### 08. Trickster — The Counterfeit Seal

**Focus:** Misdirection and investigative judgment.  
**Premise:** Recover a stolen Guild seal hidden among actors posing as merchants.

1. **Demonstration:** Examine conflicting clues and identify likely suspects.
2. **Complication:** A misleading witness directs suspicion toward an innocent merchant.
3. **Final Test:** Recover the seal through distraction, exchange, or nonviolent deception.

**Passing objective:** Solve the mystery without unnecessary harm or falsely condemning an innocent person.

#### 09. Phantom — The Vanishing Gallery

**Focus:** Concealment, timing, and evasion.  
**Premise:** Recover an object from an illuminated gallery patrolled by enchanted sentries.

1. **Demonstration:** Cross the first section using concealment, movement, and timing.
2. **Complication:** Moving lanterns eliminate some formerly safe shadows.
3. **Final Test:** Retrieve the object and escape without sustained detection.

**Passing objective:** Demonstrate clever concealment, timing, and escape using **existing** abilities. Phantom techniques must not be prerequisites.

### Luminary

#### 10. Dawnbringer — The Dying Beacon

**Focus:** Courage, radiance, and protection.  
**Premise:** Defend an ancient beacon from simulated shadow creatures.

1. **Demonstration:** Use available Luminary abilities to defend the beacon and its attendant.
2. **Complication:** The beacon weakens while additional threats approach the attendant.
3. **Final Test:** Restore the beacon and protect the attendant from the remaining danger.

**Passing objective:** Balance offensive action with the duty to protect others.

#### 11. Sanctifier — The Broken Seal

**Focus:** Purification and discernment.  
**Premise:** Investigate minor magical contamination threatening a Guild sanctuary.

1. **Demonstration:** Examine affected objects and distinguish magical contamination from normal damage.
2. **Complication:** A harmless object has a similar magical signature and creates a false lead.
3. **Final Test:** Apply a basic purification rite to stabilize the actual source without harming occupants or valuable artifacts.

**Passing objective:** Correctly diagnose, contain, and act responsibly with magic.

#### 12. Spiritkeeper — A Name Unspoken

**Focus:** Compassion and spiritual investigation.  
**Premise:** Resolve a supernatural disturbance at an abandoned Guild residence.

1. **Demonstration:** Study spiritual traces, records, and the home's history.
2. **Complication:** The haunting proves to be a spirit seeking aid rather than an enemy.
3. **Final Test:** Identify and honor the spirit's unfinished request through an appropriate action or rite.

**Passing objective:** Resolve the disturbance respectfully without forcibly banishing a peaceful spirit.

### Arcanist

#### 13. Elementalist — The Three Crucibles

**Focus:** Elemental control and inventive reasoning.  
**Premise:** Complete three elemental puzzles using basic magic and available environmental materials.

1. **Demonstration:** Use a basic elemental Weavebolt to activate a training mechanism.
2. **Complication:** An unexpected magical reaction makes raw power ineffective or dangerous.
3. **Final Test:** Stabilize the last crucible through a carefully controlled elemental effect and available materials.

**Passing objective:** Apply elemental magic safely and creatively. Knowledge of multiple elements is **not** required.

#### 14. Runecaster — The Failing Gate

**Focus:** Runic literacy and magical troubleshooting.  
**Premise:** Diagnose a malfunctioning rune-powered Guild gate.

1. **Demonstration:** Identify the function of three existing runes using basic Runic Insight.
2. **Complication:** A damaged rune makes the obvious activation sequence unsafe.
3. **Final Test:** Reconfigure supplied training sigils and activate the gate safely.

**Passing objective:** Understand the system, choose a logical repair sequence, and avoid reckless activation.

#### 15. Spellbinder — The Unruly Conduit

**Focus:** Magical control and threat diagnosis.  
**Premise:** Shut down an unstable magical conduit while several spells interfere with the chamber.

1. **Demonstration:** Recognize the nature and direction of incoming magical effects.
2. **Complication:** A dramatic-looking spell is illusory while a subtler effect threatens the mechanism.
3. **Final Test:** Redirect, interrupt through provided controls, or isolate the harmful magical flow.

**Passing objective:** Diagnose accurately and contain magic without uncontrolled destruction. No advanced counterspell is needed.

### Resonant

#### 16. Warcaller — The Drummer's Crossing

**Focus:** Battlefield communication and coordination.  
**Premise:** Lead three novice Guild recruits escorting a supply wagon across a contested bridge.

1. **Demonstration:** Establish a simple communication and movement plan.
2. **Complication:** Loud noise or a simulated attack interrupts the signals.
3. **Final Test:** Regroup the recruits and get the wagon to safety.

**Passing objective:** Restore effective communication and group coordination under changing conditions.

#### 17. Echo Sage — The Whispering Archive

**Focus:** Resonance, listening, and evidence.  
**Premise:** Find a missing Guild record in an archive filled with misleading echoes.

1. **Demonstration:** Interpret sound direction, vibrations, and acoustic clues.
2. **Complication:** Enchanted surfaces repeat false or delayed sounds.
3. **Final Test:** Identify the authentic source, find the record, and explain the reasoning.

**Passing objective:** Investigate carefully and distinguish genuine clues from misleading echoes.

#### 18. Harmonist — Three Unsteady Hearts

**Focus:** Cooperation and emotional resilience.  
**Premise:** Help three Guild trainees with conflicting priorities complete a shared task.

1. **Demonstration:** Listen and identify the cause of their conflict.
2. **Complication:** One trainee grows frightened or frustrated and refuses to continue.
3. **Final Test:** Re-establish enough **voluntary** cooperation for the group to complete its objective.

**Passing objective:** Build genuine teamwork without coercion or magical control of emotions.

## 3. Race and Lineage Customization Framework

Use **one optional lineage modifier** per trial. A modifier can adjust scenery, an environmental route, a clue, an NPC interaction, or an alternate means of reaching the same goal. It should never add an entirely new success requirement or grant an automatic special power.

> **Roster integrity:** All twelve approved base races have exactly five listed lineages. This section does not add excluded Beastkin, Skyborn, or Tidekin variants.

### Human — Regional heritage

Human variations represent culture, experience, and training—not fixed biological powers.

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Highlander | Elevated checkpoint, mountain passage, or rope-assisted crossing |
| Coastlander | Harbor, dockside obstacle, maritime signals, or shipboard setting |
| Plainsfolk | Caravan route, open crossroads, or distant landmark puzzle |
| Dune Dweller | Supplies puzzle involving water, shade, heat, or travel provisioning |
| Frostlander | Icy path, winter equipment, or cold-weather rescue scenario |

### Elf — Natural and magical sensitivity

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| High Elf | Ancient magical inscription or enchanted mechanism providing an optional clue |
| Wood Elf | Forest checkpoint, canopy path, or dense vegetation |
| Moon Elf | Moonlit setting with reflections or faint, concealed markings |
| Deep Elf | Dim tunnel or underground chamber |
| Frost Elf | Frosted ruin, frozen artifact, or cold-weather route |

### Dwarf — Endurance and material knowledge

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Mountain Dwarf | Difficult stone passage, weighted gate, or rocky incline |
| Hill Dwarf | Uneven ground, hillside checkpoint, or balance/stability problem |
| Deep Dwarf | Cave junction with environmental or geological clues |
| Ember Dwarf | Controlled forge area, warm chamber, or harmless heat source |
| Crystal Dwarf | Mineral resonance, patterned crystals, or vibrating stone mechanism |

### Halfling — Ingenuity and mobility

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Hillfoot | Terraced slope or uneven footpath |
| Woodfoot | Wooded corridor, low branches, or compact climbing route |
| Riverfoot | Stepping stones, shallow stream, or small water crossing |
| Marshfoot | Soft ground, unstable footing, or reed-covered route |
| Cavefoot | Short tunnel or concealed passage with a fair, wider alternative |

### Orcborn — Physical resilience and environmental adaptation

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Mountain Orc | Heavy barricade or sturdy obstacle that can be moved or bypassed |
| Steppe Orc | Pursuit section or extended route where tactical pacing matters |
| Ash Orc | Controlled ash-covered path, soot-obscured clue, or volcanic-themed zone |
| Marsh Orc | Shallow water, mud, or unstable boardwalk |
| Frost Orc | Windswept ridge or simulated winter hazard |

### Beastkin — Animal lineage traits

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Wolfkin | Conflicting scent trails or traces of a moving group |
| Foxkin | Decoy, misleading footprints, or an NPC whose motives can be read |
| Catkin | Narrow ledge, balance segment, or precision-positioning option |
| Rabbitkin | Short jumping course or sudden escape-route opportunity |
| Bearkin | Rescue involving a heavy obstacle or sheltering someone vulnerable |

**Approved Beastkin only:** Wolfkin, Foxkin, Catkin, Rabbitkin, Bearkin.

### Drakari — Draconic ancestry

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Wyrmkin | Reinforced gate, pressure plate, or heavy obstacle |
| Serpentkin | Winding passage or movement challenge with alternate routes |
| Drakekin | Close-quarters encounter or defensive positioning opportunity |
| Crystaldrake | Magical disturbance resonating through nearby crystals or stone |
| Thornscale | Controlled contact hazard or obstacle rewarding defensive responses |

**Anatomy limit:** No assumed wings or unrestricted breath attacks; appearance color does not grant elemental affinity.

### Ashborn — Environmental and supernatural awareness

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Cinderborn | Controlled embers, heated stones, or warm chamber |
| Duskhorn | Shifting shadows, hidden observer, or low-light checkpoint |
| Paleash | Arcane fluctuation or faint magical signal |
| Thornhorn | Defensive positioning or controlled contact challenge |
| Smokeborn | Light smoke, obscured markings, or visibility puzzle |

### Sylph — Gliding and sensory adaptation

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Butterfly Sylph | Colored panels, visual distractions, or brief controlled-glide option |
| Moth Sylph | Dim section with subtle light and sound clues |
| Dragonfly Sylph | Winding route, quick changes of direction, or controlled descent |
| Cicada Sylph | Rhythmic vibration, repeating sound, or acoustic clue |
| Lacewing Sylph | Delicate precision placement or controlled landing task |

**Movement limit:** Gliding only; no unrestricted flight. Ground-accessible alternatives remain available.

### Tidekin — Aquatic heritage

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Riverkin | Shallow current, water-driven mechanism, or river navigation clue |
| Sharkkin | Movement disturbances in shallow water or aquatic tracking clue |
| Reefkin | Color patterns, shoreline cover, or camouflage puzzle |
| Raykin | Flowing obstacle route or tight channel with turning options |
| Deepkin | Dim cistern, underwater-themed chamber, or low-light investigation |

**Accessibility limit:** Every trial remains completable on land. Water is an optional opportunity, not a mandatory swimming requirement. **Eelkin is not in the approved roster.**

### Skyborn — Avian ancestry

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Hawkin | Elevated observation point or distant tracking marker |
| Eaglekin | Long-distance visual clue or wide-area vantage point |
| Falconkin | Timed descent, swift repositioning, or precision approach |
| Owlkin | Dark watchtower, quiet observation route, or low-light clue |
| Ravenkin | Sequence puzzle, remembered conversation, or recall-based clue |

**Movement limit:** Wing-assisted movement and controlled descent are permitted; unrestricted flight is not. **Crowkin, Heronkin, Kingfisherkin, Vulturekin, and Albatrosskin are not in the approved roster.**

### Forgeborn — Constructed ancestry

| Lineage | Trial variation |
|---|---|
| Stoneforged | Stone mechanism, heavy gate, or structural-stability problem |
| Ironforged | Forged chains, reinforced barrier, or defensive mechanism |
| Bronzeforged | Fine mechanical lock, gear puzzle, or precision-controlled device |
| Woodforged | Livingwood corridor, root-shaped mechanism, or restoration clue |
| Crystalforged | Resonating crystals, refracted light, or magical energy pattern |

**Construct rules:** Use standard health, injury, and recovery mechanics unless separately finalized racial rules explicitly change them.

## 5. Consistent Cross-Table Administration

### Standard and Field Trials

- **Standard Guild Trial:** A repeatable, controlled training assignment, available through authorized Guild trainers and GMs.
- **Field Trial:** The same defined objective and rubric embedded in a live world quest. The outcome may also alter the persistent world's state.
- Field consequences are **recorded separately** from trial certification. A world event is not an extra barrier to passing.
- A player may undertake a trial during a normal three-hour session, rather than needing a separate booking.
- Other adventurers can contribute, but the applicant must demonstrate their own judgment and class ability.

### Trial record template


### Example completed record

**Race / Lineage:** Beastkin / Bearkin  
**Base class:** Vanguard  
**Trial:** The Last Lantern  
**Specialization:** Guardian  
**Variation:** Fallen beam can be used for protective shelter

| Criterion | Example result |
|---|---|
| Objective | Passed — apprentice and lantern recovered |
| Class Mastery | Passed — defensive positioning and techniques used |
| Adaptability | Passed — adjusted when the route became blocked |
| Judgment | Passed — prioritized apprentice's safety |

**Certification:** Guardian specialization granted; **Protector's Oath** Discipline Trait unlocked; Guardian advanced skill tree made available. Guild rank stays unchanged.

### GM checklist before and after a trial

**Before:**

- Verify at least five universal class SP have been invested.
- Verify the chosen specialization is one of the class's three approved options.
- Select the canonical trial and optionally one lineage modifier.
- Confirm no trial obstacle requires an as-yet-unlocked specialization skill.
- Confirm a fair alternative exists for body-size, travel, aquatic, and aerial conditions.

**During:**

- Present the same central mission and three-stage challenge.
- Allow legitimate alternate solutions.
- Record evidence for the four standard assessment criteria.
- Do not reward a lineage with automatic success or alter its base difficulty.

**After:**

- Record result, date, GM, applicant, and selected variation.
- On success, unlock the proper Discipline Trait and advanced tree.
- On failure, provide a meaningful remedial step or later retry.
- If a Field Trial changes the shared world, record that effect separately.

## 6. Certification and World-Building Rewards

After passing, the Guild may award a **digital specialization endorsement**, an optional **physical certificate**, and/or an endorsement stamp on the adventurer's Guild card. These are recognition items, not automatic Guild rank promotions.

Field Trials can also generate persistent stories:

- A Trailblazer's **The Lost Road** opens a route other groups can use.
- A Spiritkeeper's **A Name Unspoken** resolves a haunting that affected earlier expeditions.
- A Warcaller's **The Drummer's Crossing** preserves a supply delivery whose effects appear on the world news board.

The standardized test remains comparable while the world reacts to the player's decisions.

## 7. Decisions That Are Still Proposed

These should be tested before being treated as final rules:

- Exact character level or milestone required to accumulate five universal class SP.
- Whether a trial is run entirely in training or partially as a Field Trial by default.
- Final dice-check difficulty, action economy, damage, range, and resource costs.
- Retry waiting periods, if any, and the specific retraining procedure.
- Detailed handling of group-assisted trials and individual assessment.
- Whether future cross-specialization training requires another Guild Trial.

**Locked design constraints preserved:** Six fixed classes; three established specializations per class; twelve races with five approved lineages each; every lineage can choose any class; class rank F–SS remains independent; racial skill trees are separate from class specialization certification.
