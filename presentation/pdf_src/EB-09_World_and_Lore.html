Purpose

The starting state of the shared world that every table plays in. All content here is fictional seed content for the pilot and can be rewritten; the website stores it and updates it as sessions are approved.

Regions and places DRAFT

Place Region Status Summary Lore / threat
Emberfall Village The Heartlands peaceful Farming village famous for its harvest festival and its terrible ale.
Highmere The Heartlands peaceful Walled city and home of the Adventurers Guild Hall. Highmere grew around the Guild Hall, a tower of pale stone whose great bell rings whenever a contract is fulfilled.
Northern Watchtower The Frostmarch destroyed Guild outpost that stopped responding three weeks ago. The last message from the Watchtower was a single word: "Gate". Threat: Unknown attackers
Old King's Road The Frostmarch threatened The northern highway. Merchants refuse to travel it since the Watchtower fell. Threat: Unpatrolled since the Watchtower fell
Silverfen The Glimmering Marsh peaceful A marsh village where strange crystals were recently unearthed. Fisherfolk of Silverfen speak of lights beneath the water on moonless nights.
The Ashen Road The Heartlands threatened Trade road scarred by an old wildfire. Caravans have been vanishing. Charred oaks line the road for twenty miles. Merchants say the smoke never fully cleared. Threat: Bandit activity · missing caravans
The Black Hollow The Northern Ruins unknown A ravine beyond the northern ruins where strange lights have appeared. Guild scouts sent to the Hollow have not returned. Threat: Unknown
Whisperstone Mines The Greyridge threatened Abandoned dwarven mines. Miners report voices in the deep tunnels. Threat: Something stirs in the lower galleries
The Crimson Peaks The Northern Ruins unknown Red-stoned mountains at the edge of the known world. Old songs say a wyrm sleeps beneath the peaks, sealed by five sigils. Threat: Legendary
The Sunken Abbey The Glimmering Marsh unknown A drowned monastery whose bells still ring at low tide.

Undiscovered places (the Crimson Peaks and the Sunken Abbey) stay hidden from players until a party finds them.

The Crimson Wyrm raid DRAFT

  • State: foreshadowed. "The guild has recovered three of the five ancient sigils. Something stirs beyond the gate."
  • Unlocks automatically when Ancient sigils recovered reaches 5. A Chronicle proclamation is published and every player is notified.
  • Stages: Dormant → Foreshadowed → Unlocked → Registration → Active → Resolved.
  • Set up as a two-sided raid (EB-07).

The Chronicle (opening news) DRAFT

The Northern Watchtower Has Fallen (world news) — Three weeks have passed since the signal fires of the Northern Watchtower went dark. A Guild courier returned yesterday with grim news: the outer wall is breached and the garrison is gone. There were no bodies. Until the road is secured, the Guild advises all merchants to avoid the Old King's Road. Adventurers of any rank may accept scouting contracts at the Quest Board.

Unusual Crystals Discovered (discovery) — A party returning from Silverfen carried a shard of crystal that hums faintly when held near running water. The Guild's scholars have begun their study. Further samples are wanted; contracts will be posted soon.

The Black Hollow Stirs (world news) — Shepherds in the northern foothills report pale green lights rising from the Black Hollow after dusk. Two Guild scouts were dispatched last week. Neither has returned. The Guild is assembling a party of experienced adventurers.

The Founding Guild Charter Is Open (guild proclamation) — By order of the Guild Masters: the charter of the Everbound Adventurers Guild is open. Those who register during the founding season will bear the title Founding Adventurer for as long as the Guild stands.

A Word from Quartermaster Bram (npc announcement) — "Rope, lanterns, draughts — those I can still get. But my ironwood shields come up the Ashen Road, and nothing has come up the Ashen Road in a fortnight. Clear the road and I'll stock the good stuff again." — Bram, Quartermaster of the Highmere Guild Hall
