Version: 0.1 — first playable draft · Date: November 23, 2026 Status: Proposed for playtesting. Every number here is a starting guess. Builds on: Complete Character Progression Compendium v1.3 (classes, specializations, racial trees, synergies) and Guild Trials v1.0.

One rule to remember: roll d20 + attribute + training, and meet or beat the number.

1. The six attributes

Each attribute is a modifier from −1 to +5 that you add to rolls. There are no separate 3–18 scores.

Attribute What it measures Skills
Might Strength, lifting, breaking, heavy weapons Athletics, Force
Grace Agility, balance, aim, light weapons, Guard Acrobatics, Stealth, Finesse
Vigor Toughness, stamina, extra HP each level Endurance
Wits Reasoning, memory, study; Arcanist power Arcana, Lore, Investigation, Craft
Insight Awareness, intuition, care; Luminary power Perception, Empathy, Survival, Medicine
Presence Force of personality; Resonant power Persuasion, Deception, Performance, Command

2. The 18 skills

You are trained in a skill or not. Trained skills add your Training bonus (+2 at level 1, see the level table).

3. Building a character

  1. Choose a race. Gain its HP bonus, +1 to one attribute and its movement.
  2. Choose a lineage. Gain the free lineage trait and one trained skill (table in section 5).
  3. Choose a class. Gain its HP, Guard, +1 to its primary attribute, two trained Resists, its trained skills and its four starting abilities.
  4. Spend 6 attribute points. Every attribute starts at 0. Each point adds +1, up to +3 before race and class bonuses. You may lower one attribute to −1 for one extra point. Highest possible at level 1: +4.
  5. Work out HP and Guard (section 6).
  6. Pick starting gear: your class weapons, armor if your class uses it, an adventurer's pack and 50 gold.

  • Health (HP) at level 1 = 10 + race bonus + class HP + Vigor. Each new level adds class HP per level + Vigor (minimum +1).
    • Example: Dwarf Vanguard with Vigor +2 → 10 + 4 + 12 + 2 = 28 HP. Sylph Arcanist with Vigor 0 → 10 + 0 + 6 + 0 = 16 HP.
  • Guard is how hard you are to hit (like armor class). A shield adds +1.
  • Ability DC (what enemies roll against for your abilities) = 8 + Training + your power attribute (Arcanist: Wits · Luminary: Insight · Resonant: Presence · everyone else: their primary).
  • Weapons: Light d6 (dagger, sling, hand axe) · Standard d8 (sword, spear, bow, staff) · Heavy d10 (two-handed: greataxe, warhammer, longbow). Add Might for heavy and most melee weapons, Grace for light and ranged ones.

7. Rolling

Check: d20 + attribute + Training (if trained) vs a Difficulty.

Difficulty Number
Easy 8
Standard 12
Hard 15
Heroic 18
Legendary 21
  • Edge: roll two d20s, keep the higher. Strain: keep the lower. Edge and Strain cancel; they never stack.
  • Help: one ally with a good reason gives you Edge.
  • Group check: everyone rolls; the group succeeds if at least half succeed.
  • Resist (like a saving throw): d20 + attribute (+ Training if your class is trained in that Resist) vs the effect's DC.
  • Natural 20 on a check: a great success with an extra benefit. Natural 1: a complication. You still succeed if the total beats the Difficulty, but something goes wrong.
  • Passive Perception = 10 + Perception total; the GM uses it for things you'd notice without trying.

8. Combat

Initiative: d20 + Grace, highest first; on a tie, players go first.

On your turn:

  • Move: up to your move in squares.
  • Action: one of Attack, use an ability, Dash (move again), Help, Defend (+2 Guard and Edge on Resists until your next turn), use an item, or make a check.
  • Reaction: once per round, on anyone's turn: an Opportunity Strike when an enemy leaves your reach, or a reaction ability.
  • Talking, drawing a weapon and dropping an item are free.

Attack: d20 + attribute + Training vs the target's Guard. Damage: weapon or ability dice + attribute.

  • Natural 20: critical hit, roll the damage dice twice. Natural 1: miss.

Ability uses: Core Techniques are unlimited. "1/encounter" abilities, Signature abilities and racial Tier III skills come back when the encounter ends.

Conditions:

Condition Effect Ends
Exposed Attacks against you have Edge End of your next turn
Staggered No reactions; Strain on your next attack End of your next turn
Pinned Move is 0 End of your next turn, or escape (Athletics/Acrobatics vs DC)
Prone Melee attacks against you have Edge; standing costs half your move You stand up
Frightened Strain on checks and attacks while the source is in sight Resist (Insight) at end of each turn
Hidden Unseen: your next attack has Edge You attack, make noise, or are found
Poisoned Strain on attacks and checks Resist (Vigor) at end of each turn, or Medicine
Shielded +2 Guard Start of the shielder's next turn
Downed At 0 HP: prone, can't act; make Last Breath rolls Any healing, or stabilized

Downed and Last Breath

At 0 HP you are Downed. At the start of each of your turns, roll a d20: 10 or more is a success, 9 or less is a failure. Taking damage while Downed counts as one failure.

  • 3 successes: you are stable at 0 HP.
  • Any healing, or an ally stabilizing you (Medicine vs Standard 12, or Rite of Clarity), brings you back.
  • 3 failures: you have Fallen. You are out for the rest of the encounter and wake at 1 HP afterwards with a Lingering Wound: a story tag the GM uses until your next session.
  • No permanent death unless the player chooses a Heroic Sacrifice.

9. Recovery

  • Recovery Dice: you have one per level, of your class's Recovery die.
  • Breather (about 10 minutes in the story; up to two per session): spend any number of Recovery Dice, rolling each and adding Vigor, and heal that much.
  • Between sessions: HP, Recovery Dice and all uses reset when you next check in.

11. Starting abilities with numbers (first pass)

These add numbers to the 24 starting abilities from the Class Compendium. Effects at L5 and L9 grow as noted.

Vanguard

  • Iron Resolve — Edge on Resists against being pushed or knocked prone.
  • Shieldbreak — Weapon attack; on a hit, push the target 1 square OR it has Strain on attacks against anyone but you until your next turn.
  • Stand Before Me (reaction, 1/encounter) — When an ally within 1 square is hit, take the hit instead and reduce its damage by 1d8 + Vigor.
  • Siegeborn — Edge on checks to find structural weak points, defensible positions or ways through barriers.

Wildstrider

  • Keen Instinct — +2 Perception to notice tracks, ambushes and hazards; you can't be surprised while conscious.
  • Skirmisher's Strike — Weapon attack, then move up to 2 squares without provoking.
  • Predator's Snare (1/encounter) — Target Resists (Grace) vs your ability DC or is Pinned; allies have Edge against it until your next turn.
  • Pathfinder — Edge on Survival to find routes, trails and safer paths.

Shadowblade

  • Exploit Weakness — Once per turn, +1d6 damage against a target that is Exposed, unaware, or next to one of your allies (+2d6 at L5, +3d6 at L9).
  • Feint & Slip — Weapon attack, then move up to 2 squares to cover without provoking.
  • Veilstep (1/encounter) — Move up to 3 squares to a spot out of an enemy's sight; you are Hidden.
  • Underhand — Edge on Finesse with locks, traps and hidden compartments.

Luminary

  • Guiding Flame — Edge on Medicine to diagnose and on Perception to sense spirits or corruption.
  • Radiant Hand — Give an ally within 6 squares Shielded (+2 Guard), OR spell attack (Insight) for 1d8 radiant (2d8 at L5, 3d8 at L9).
  • Wellspring (1/encounter) — An ally within 6 squares heals 1d8 + Insight (+1d8 at L5 and L9) OR ends one minor condition.
  • Rite of Clarity — Stabilize a Downed ally without a check; purify a small tainted object; Edge on Medicine checks.

Arcanist

  • Arcane Sense — Sense active magic within 6 squares.
  • Weavebolt — Spell attack (Wits), range 12, 1d8 (2d8 at L5, 3d8 at L9). Pick: fire (ignites), frost (target moves 1 less), lightning (no reactions), force (push 1).
  • Sigilburst (1/encounter) — 2-square radius within 8 squares; each creature Resists (Grace) vs your DC: 2d6 damage, half on a success (+1d6 at L5 and L9).
  • Runic Insight — Edge on Arcana to read runes, symbols and magical devices.

Resonant

  • Attunement — Edge on Empathy to read a group's mood; sense supernatural sounds.
  • Harmonic Cue — An ally within 6 squares adds 1d4 to their next roll, OR a target Resists (Presence) vs your DC or takes 1d6 sonic and has Strain on its next attack.
  • Grand Refrain (reaction, 1/encounter) — An ally within 6 squares rerolls a failed roll and adds +2.
  • Echo of Words — Edge on Performance; make your voice carry; recall faint sounds you heard.

12. The session workflow

  1. Check in — Scan your guild card. The GM tablet loads your sheet; HP and uses reset.
  2. Session zero — Safety tools, quest briefing, who's playing what.
  3. Frame a scene — The GM sets the scene: explore, talk, solve or fight.
  4. Act and roll — Say what you do. If it's risky, roll d20 + attribute + training vs the Difficulty.
  5. Encounter — Initiative, then rounds: Move, Action, Reaction. Ends on defeat, retreat, surrender or objective.
  6. Breather — Between scenes: spend Recovery Dice to heal; plan the next move.
  7. Finale — The big choice that can change the world.
  8. Wrap-up — The GM records XP, gold, loot, renown and world changes; a second GM approves.

A three-hour session usually has 4–6 scenes and 2–3 encounters. Between sessions players level up (SP, RP, attributes), run errands, craft, trade at the Auction Hall and book Guild Trials.

14. Decisions for you

  • Dice: d20 + attribute + training vs a target number (closest to D&D).
  • Attributes: Six attributes. The website currently has four (Might, Grace, Wits, Spirit) and switches with one ruleset edit.
  • Death: No permanent death without the player's consent; falling in battle means a lasting wound in the story.
  • HP between sessions: Full reset at the start of each session; injuries carry over only as story.
  • Numbers: Starting HP, Guard and damage here are first guesses to playtest.
